# Days off
Decided 10 Nov, so meals on school and work days can be compared with weekends and holidays.
- Saturdays and Sundays.
- US federal holidays, worked out for any year with no list to keep up: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day,
  Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving and
  Christmas. One on a Saturday is also off on the Friday before, and one on a Sunday on the Monday after (the observed day).
- The family's own (Manage → Settings → Days off): a name and a first and last day, e.g. 'Winter break, 21 Dec to
  2 Jan' or 'Teacher day, 13 Feb'. Up to 60, each up to 30 days long, saved with the settings save bar like the rest
  of Settings (okf/design/components/settings-save.md). Any family member can see them; only a parent (PIN) changes them.
- A day that is several of these says the family's name first, then the holiday, then the weekday name.
