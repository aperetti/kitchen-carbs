# Estimated photo label
This includes photos generated from the name and grams alone, with no photo of the food. Because generated volume can be wrong, the label stays until a real photo is uploaded.
- Where (decided 27 Oct): not as a tag on the food cards, which the parent found cluttered; it is shown in the card's
  ⓘ details, in Manage, and on the photo check screens.
