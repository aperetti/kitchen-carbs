# Estimated photo label
This includes photos generated from the name and grams alone, with no photo of the food, and a favorite's plate
photo combined from its foods' photos (decided 30 Oct), labelled in the favorite's ⓘ. Because generated volume can be wrong, the label stays until a real photo is uploaded.
A new portion showing its parent portion's photo unchanged (decided 6 Nov) is labelled the same way, worded 'Same photo as
another portion', since the amount in it is the parent's, not this portion's.
- Where (decided 27 Oct): not as a tag on the food cards, which the parent found cluttered; it is shown in the card's
  ⓘ details, in Manage, and on the photo check screens.
