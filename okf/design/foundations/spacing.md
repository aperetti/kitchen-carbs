# Spacing
8 px base; page gutter 32 px on tablet; touch targets ≥ 56 px; corners 4 px (food cards 14 px, their name pill 10 px,
decided 27 Oct for a softer photo grid); no shadows, except the weight slider's
thumb (decided 27 Oct), which follows Apple's slider guidelines as a physical control.

## Layout by width
- Wide (≥ 1400 px, e.g. a 2K monitor): three columns. Search and list on the left (320 px), the plate photo
  in the centre at full 4:3 (never cropped, never taller than the window), food details and the current plate on the right (380 px).
- Tablet landscape: list + detail, with the photo above the details and the plate bar at the bottom.
- Portrait / phone: one column.
