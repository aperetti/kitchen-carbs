# Small screens and large text
Decided 29 Oct: on a small phone or with the text made bigger, the plate's carbs number sat on top of the name and the
dry / cooked line. The carbs must always be readable on their own.
- Checked at 320 px wide and at 260 px (a 390 px phone at 150 % text or zoom).
- The phone plate uses the same stacked layout as a thin tablet pane: photo beside the name and portion, the carbs on
  their own line under them, then the slider and weight across the full width.
- Text columns may shrink (no fixed minimums) and long names wrap; nothing in a row is wider than its column.
- At the thinnest widths the photo shrinks and the 'Weight' label hides (the field keeps its 'g'); the carbs never shrink below 26 px.
- The scale strip wraps its buttons to a second line instead of squeezing the reading.
- The four tabs share the top bar evenly; none runs off the edge.
- No sideways scrolling on any screen.
