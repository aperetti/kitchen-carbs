# Avoid
Purple/indigo gradients, glow/aurora, emoji as icons, cards around everything, coloured side accent bars,
meaningless status dots, Inter, glossy pill buttons, three-icon feature grids, and the 'new default'
of cream + serif + terracotta.
Sources: thefountaininstitute.com/blog/signs-vibe-coded-ui, codemyspec.com/blog/vibe-coded-websites-look-the-same
